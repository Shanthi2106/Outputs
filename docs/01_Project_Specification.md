# AI-Powered Parent Assistant for Autism
## Project Specification Document

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** January 2026
**Project Lead:** Sai Prashanthi Vemula

---

## Executive Summary

This project aims to develop an AI-powered educational assistant that helps parents and caregivers of children on the autism spectrum understand complex autism-related terminology. By leveraging Natural Language Processing (NLP), the assistant will translate clinical and academic language into clear, empathetic, plain-language explanations, empowering parents to confidently interpret reports and communicate effectively with professionals.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

### 1.1 Target Users
- **Primary:** Parents and caregivers of children on the autism spectrum
- **Secondary:** Guardians and family members seeking to understand autism terminology
- **Context:** Healthcare education / Special education (non-clinical)

### 1.2 When the Problem Occurs
Parents encounter complex terminology in several critical situations:
- After receiving an autism diagnosis or assessment report
- While reading therapy notes, IEP documents, or school evaluations
- During meetings with therapists, educators, or specialists
- When researching autism-related information online

### 1.3 Current Situation
Parents currently attempt to understand autism-related terminology through:
- **Internet searches:** Blogs, forums, medical websites (fragmented and inconsistent)
- **Clinical reports:** Often contain minimal explanation
- **Professional consultations:** Limited appointment time for clarification
- **Social media groups:** Informal and variable quality information

**Key Issues with Current Approaches:**
- Information is fragmented across multiple sources
- Content is often overly technical or clinical
- Inconsistent explanations across different sources
- No centralized, trusted resource

### 1.4 Core Problem
**Parents struggle to understand autism-related terminology due to the use of clinical and academic language, making it difficult to confidently interpret reports and communicate effectively with professionals.**

---

## 2. Impact Analysis

### 2.1 Time Cost
- Significant time spent researching individual terms online
- Repeated searches required for each new report or document
- Research time reduces time available for family and caregiving activities

### 2.2 Money Cost
- Indirect costs from additional consultations or follow-up meetings
- Potential purchase of unnecessary resources due to misunderstanding
- Productivity loss or missed work hours due to emotional stress

### 2.3 Quality/Accuracy Issues
- Online information may be outdated, contradictory, or overly clinical
- Inconsistent explanations lead to confusion rather than clarity
- Difficulty distinguishing educational information from medical advice

### 2.4 Opportunity Cost
Parents could instead focus on:
- Better engagement with their child
- More productive collaboration with educators and therapists
- Supportive activities rather than terminology research

### 2.5 Emotional Impact
- Medical and educational terms are complex and intimidating
- Misinterpretation can increase anxiety or misunderstanding
- Parents may feel unprepared during important discussions or decisions
- Lack of confidence in communicating with professionals

---

## 3. MVP Goal

**Enable parents to quickly understand autism-related terminology through clear, empathetic, and plain-language explanations using an AI-powered educational assistant.**

---

## 4. MVP Scope

### 4.1 In-Scope Capabilities

#### Capability 1: AI-Powered Term Explanations
- Provide explanations of common autism-related terms
- Use simple, parent-friendly language
- Ensure clarity and accessibility for non-clinical audiences

#### Capability 2: Contextual Clarification
- Clarify terminology as it appears in real documents
- Support short excerpts from:
  - IEPs (Individualized Education Programs)
  - Therapy notes
  - Assessment reports
  - School evaluations

#### Capability 3: Conversational Interaction
- Enable parents to ask follow-up questions
- Provide simplified explanations upon request
- Offer practical examples to illustrate concepts
- Support multi-turn conversations for deeper understanding

### 4.2 Explicitly Out of Scope

#### Item 1: Medical Advice
- No medical diagnosis
- No treatment recommendations
- No therapy advice
- *Rationale:* Maintains clear boundaries between education and clinical practice

#### Item 2: Data Storage
- No storage of sensitive personal information
- No processing of medical data
- *Rationale:* Ensures privacy and compliance with healthcare regulations

#### Item 3: System Integration
- No real-time integration with healthcare systems
- No connection to school information systems
- *Rationale:* Reduces complexity and compliance requirements for MVP

---

## 5. Success Criteria

### 5.1 Target Users
- Parents and guardians of children on the autism spectrum
- Caregivers seeking to better understand autism terminology

### 5.2 Success Indicators

#### Behavioral Indicators
- Parents are able to understand and explain autism-related terms in their own words
- Parents actively use the assistant when reviewing reports or preparing for meetings
- Parents demonstrate increased confidence in professional communications

#### Measurable Outcomes
- **Understanding:** Parent feedback indicating improved comprehension and reduced confusion
- **Professional Engagement:** Reduced need for repeated clarification from therapists, educators, or specialists
- **Sustained Usage:** Continued usage across multiple documents or time periods
- **Satisfaction:** Positive user feedback and testimonials

### 5.3 Validation Methods
- User surveys measuring understanding and confidence levels
- Usage analytics tracking frequency and patterns
- Follow-up interviews with parents
- Feedback from professionals on parent engagement quality

---

## 6. Key Principles

### 6.1 Clarity
- Use plain language, free from jargon
- Provide definitions that are immediately understandable
- Break down complex concepts into simple components

### 6.2 Empathy
- Acknowledge the emotional journey of parents
- Use compassionate, supportive tone
- Validate concerns while providing clear information

### 6.3 Trust
- Base explanations on evidence-based understanding
- Maintain consistency across all interactions
- Clearly distinguish educational information from medical advice

### 6.4 Accessibility
- Design for users with varying levels of technical literacy
- Support diverse learning styles (definitions, examples, analogies)
- Ensure responsive and intuitive interface

---

## 7. Constraints and Assumptions

### 7.1 Constraints
- Must not provide medical, diagnostic, or treatment advice
- Must not store or process sensitive personal/medical data
- Must maintain clear educational (non-clinical) positioning

### 7.2 Assumptions
- Parents have basic internet access and digital literacy
- Parents will use the tool proactively when encountering terminology
- Simplified explanations will reduce anxiety and improve understanding
- Conversational AI can effectively translate clinical language

## 8. Next Steps

2. **Technical Design:** Define NLP approach and conversational AI architecture
