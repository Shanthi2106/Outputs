INDIVIDUALIZED EDUCATION PROGRAM (IEP)
Student: John Doe
Date: January 2026

PRESENT LEVELS OF PERFORMANCE:
John is a 7-year-old student with autism spectrum disorder. He demonstrates strengths in visual learning and shows interest in technology. John has challenges with social skills and executive function.

ANNUAL GOALS:
1. Communication: John will improve his ability to initiate conversations with peers using AAC device, with 80% success rate.
2. Social Skills: John will demonstrate turn-taking during structured play activities with adult support.
3. Behavior: John will use self-regulation strategies when experiencing sensory overload or signs of meltdown.

ACCOMMODATIONS:
- Extended time on tests (time-and-a-half)
- Preferential seating near teacher
- Visual schedules for daily routine
- Access to sensory break area
- Use of noise-canceling headphones during transitions

SERVICES:
- ABA Therapy: 3 hours per week
- Speech Therapy: 2 sessions per week (30 minutes each)
- Occupational Therapy: 1 session per week (45 minutes) for sensory processing support

John also experiences echolalia, repeating phrases from videos. He benefits from structured activities that address his executive function challenges, particularly with task initiation and organization. Stimming behaviors (hand-flapping) are noted but not targeted for reduction as they serve a self-regulation purpose.
